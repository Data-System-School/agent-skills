# Pass 2 — the careful read

## Before you read

This rung runs its own commit cycle. On entry the notes file reads `pass: 2`,
`stage: sealed`, and while it does, the body, the figures and the proofs stay closed — and
so does any secondary account of them. Hand over the pass 2 template below, take the
reader's commit, and read after that, never before.

The gate's reasoning, what counts as a commit, the no-backdating rule and the shape of a
refusal are in [pass-1.md](pass-1.md). They govern this rung word for word, with the pass 2
slots in place of the five Cs.

## Budget and method

Up to an hour, for an experienced reader. In a new area it takes longer, or you stop
short — both are fine. Say which happened when you fill in the notes.

Read the body with care, and the references not yet read. **Not the proofs** — those belong
to pass 3, which re-derives them rather than reads them.

## What earns extra time, and why

Two things get disproportionate attention:

- **The figures, especially the graphs.** Are the axes labeled? Are results shown with
  error bars, so the conclusions are statistically significant? This is where rushed
  work separates from excellent work, and it costs a minute to check.
- **The references not yet read.** Mark the relevant ones — the cheapest way to acquire
  the missing background.

## The exit

After this pass you can summarize the main thrust **with its supporting evidence** to
someone else. The thrust alone is not enough: if you cannot also give the evidence, the
pass is not finished.

## If you still do not understand it

There are four honest causes:

1. The subject is new to you.
2. The authors used a technique you do not know.
3. The paper is badly written.
4. It is late and you are tired.

Name the fourth explicitly. It is not a joke: it is the only one of the four that is
neither a gap in you nor a defect in the paper, and its remedy is sleep, not background
reading.

Then three responses:

- Set it aside.
- Come back later.
- Persevere into [pass 3](pass-3.md).

## Divergences

Once `committed`, the skill reads and writes its own answers into the pass 2 divergence
report, slot by slot, against the reader's. The three rules are pass 1's, unchanged:

- Both answered, **differently** → discuss.
- The reader wrote `UNANSWERED`, the skill answered → discuss. Still the highest-value
  cell.
- Both **agree** → one line. No elaboration.

[pass-1.md](pass-1.md) gives the reason the third one is discipline rather than brevity;
it is not restated here. The `secondary: used` caveat carries over too: if the reader's
answers came out of a summary rather than the paper, agreement between the two of you is
not evidence of anything, and the report says so.

## Notes to append

Append this section to the paper's notes file, below the pass 1 blocks.

```markdown
## Pass 2 — my commit    <date time>

- **Main thrust, with its supporting evidence:** <!-- -->
- **Figures and graphs:** <!-- axes labeled? error bars? anything overstated? -->
- **References to go and read:** <!-- -->
- **Terms I did not understand / questions for the authors:** <!-- -->
- **Decision:** <!-- set aside / come back later / go to pass 3 -->

## Pass 2 — divergences    <date time>

<!-- Only divergences. Agreement gets one line. -->

- **Decision, revised:** <!-- unchanged, or changed — and by which divergence -->
```

Then move the frontmatter to match the revised decision, in one edit. Set aside, or come
back later → `stage: contrasted`, `pass:` left at 2. Go to pass 3 → `pass: 3` and
`stage: sealed` together, and the rung above opens with the reader's re-creation, not with
the skill's.
